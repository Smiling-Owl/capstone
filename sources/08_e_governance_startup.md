# E-governance and startup-operated DRRM services

## Source

Republic Act No. 12254. (2025). *E-Governance Act*.

https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2025/ra_12254_2025.html

## Evidence used

- Sections 3-5 cover digital government principles and apply the Act to LGUs and back-end or government-to-government operations.
- Section 6 recognizes startup support and collaboration with private entities.
- Sections 9-13 address interoperability, records and knowledge management, privacy impact, security standards, and continuity planning.
- Sections 19-21 address accessibility, mobile-friendly government websites, and alternate service modes for users without internet.
- Sections 23-25 require authorized access, risk-proportionate safeguards, periodic control testing, privacy engineering, and continuing agency responsibility for datasets.
- Section 26 permits government-private collaboration subject to accountability and good-governance rules.

## Project-context finding

The Act supports a reusable startup-operated platform for participating DRRM organizations, but it does not transfer statutory authority to the startup or prove that a prototype is interoperable or compliant. Each organization remains responsible for its government data and official decisions.
