## Evidence used

Research on emergency-management information systems supports end-user participation and attention to organizational cooperation rather than assuming adoption from technical availability. UNDRR supports consistent, comparable, and disaggregated disaster information. Philippine procurement law and World Bank GovTech guidance support evaluating lifecycle cost, provider performance, contractual duties, portability, and vendor dependency when government adopts a recurring digital service.

## Limitation

The emergency-system research is not Philippine-specific, and the World Bank document is procurement guidance rather than an impact evaluation. These sources justify adoption and service-governance conditions but do not prove that the proposed startup will reduce costs, be procured by an LGU, or improve disaster outcomes.
