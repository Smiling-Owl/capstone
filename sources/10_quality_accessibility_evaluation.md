## Evidence used

ISO/IEC 25012 distinguishes data-quality characteristics and supports separate definitions for completeness, validity, consistency, uniqueness, timeliness, and accuracy. ISO/IEC 25010:2023 provides a product-quality reference model for specifying and evaluating selected characteristics. WCAG 2.2 supplies testable Level A and AA accessibility criteria. Brooke defines the ten-item SUS scoring procedure, while Bangor and colleagues provide contextual empirical interpretation.

## Limitation

These standards and instruments do not establish universal response-time, availability, data-quality, or acceptance thresholds for this project. WCAG conformance does not by itself establish workflow usability, and a SUS score is not a percentage or proof of operational effectiveness.
