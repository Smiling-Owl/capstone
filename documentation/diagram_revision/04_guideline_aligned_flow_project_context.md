## Proposed Project Context text

The 2024 *National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Operations Center Standard Operating Procedures and Guidelines* treats disaster reporting as a progressive information lifecycle rather than a single act of documentation. At the onset of an incident, an Initial Report communicates preliminary information and may be issued even when complete supporting documentation is not yet available. As the incident develops, Progress Reports communicate changes in the prevailing situation, actions taken, and other material developments. These later updates are expected to be supported by information from identified sources and available documentation (National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council [NDRRMC], 2024, pp. 52–53).

When a particular incident-response process or phase concludes, a Terminal Report records the final available impact figures for that phase, including casualties, affected population, and damage. For continuing or larger events, Situation Reports consolidate the prevailing situation, preparedness and response actions, cumulative effects, issues and concerns, and recommendations. A Final Report subsequently provides a comprehensive account of the event from occurrence through the conclusion of response and early recovery, including outcomes, identified shortcomings, and recommendations for future incidents (NDRRMC, 2024, pp. 53–55).

Figure [X] presents a researcher-developed swimlane interpretation of this reporting lifecycle. It groups the activities into information provision, authorized DRRM review, report development and approval, and records or official dissemination. These lanes are analytical categories used by the researchers to show that information must be reviewed, clarified where necessary, developed into the appropriate report classification, and preserved with its supporting documentation. They are not official office names or a verbatim workflow prescribed by the NDRRMC.

This lifecycle informs the proposed system’s treatment of reports as evolving, traceable records. Early information may remain operationally useful even when incomplete, provided that its source, reporting time, review status, corrections, and relationship to later versions are preserved. The proposed system therefore supports human review, correction requests, report versioning, consolidation, approval, and local City SitRep preparation. It does not independently convert a local system-generated output into an official national NDRRMC issuance, and submission to external authorities remains subject to the applicable institutional process.

## Claim to avoid

Do not write that the NDRRMOC SOPG mandates the exact Purok → Barangay → City → Region → National software workflow unless another official source explicitly establishes that complete routing. The cited SOPG pages support the **report classifications and lifecycle**, while the local organizational handoffs must be supported separately by law, local policy, official forms, or interview evidence.
