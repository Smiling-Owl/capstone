# NDRRM Operations Center SOPG 2024

## Source

National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council. (2024). *National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Operations Center standard operating procedures and guidelines: 2024 edition*.

https://ndrrmc.gov.ph/attachments/article/4125/NDRRMOC_SOPG_2024_EDITION.pdf

## Evidence used

- Page 52 defines an Initial Report as preliminary information issued at incident onset and allows submission before complete documentation is available.
- Page 53 distinguishes Progress and Terminal Reports and requires later updates to be supported by source documentation.
- Pages 54-55 define the cumulative SitRep and Final Report, including situation, actions, consolidated effects, issues, outcomes, shortcomings, and recommendations.
- Page 63 organizes affected-population information by barangay, city or municipality, and province and identifies consolidated LDRRMC reports among official sources.

## Project-context finding

Disaster reporting accumulates and validates information over time. The system should preserve preliminary and verified states, provenance, corrections, geographic identifiers, and version history. A locally generated output supports SitRep preparation but does not automatically become an official national NDRRMC SitRep.
