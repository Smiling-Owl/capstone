https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.CSWP.29

https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/2023-compendium-2.pdf

Republic Act No. 12254. (2025). *E-Governance Act*.

https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2025/ra_12254_2025.html

## Evidence used

The sources support risk-based governance, protection, detection, response, recovery, backup restoration, privacy engineering, periodic control testing, and organization-level isolation. They also support testing support access and recovery rather than treating configuration, backup creation, or policy statements as proof that controls work.

## Limitation

NIST and OWASP are reference frameworks and technical guidance, not Philippine legal mandates. A prototype test covers only the declared paths and conditions; it cannot prove absolute isolation, production availability, statutory compliance, or resilience under every disaster condition.
