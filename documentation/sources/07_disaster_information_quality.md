## Evidence used

Disaster information quality is context dependent and includes completeness, validity, credibility, accuracy, currency, accessibility, interpretability, and consistency. Source traceability, update time, and access control affect whether information is usable. Systematic-review evidence also identifies unreliable communications, slow analysis, and poor interoperability as recurring limits on situational awareness.

## Limitation

These studies do not evaluate the proposed system or establish a Philippine effect size. They support design requirements and cautious claims, not guaranteed improvements in disaster decisions.
