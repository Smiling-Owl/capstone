## Evidence used

The studies identify continuing institutional, methodological, monitoring, coordination, and knowledge-management gaps in Philippine DRRM. The 2023 assessment describes loss-and-damage accounting as narrow and inconsistent and notes that localized small-scale or slow-onset effects may remain undocumented.

## Limitation

These studies provide national and selected case evidence. They do not prove that every DRRM office follows the same manual workflow or that a web platform will independently solve institutional-capacity problems.
