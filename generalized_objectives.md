# 1.3 Objectives

### General Objective
To design, develop, and evaluate a centralized, role-based Online Disaster Situation Reports Generation and Record Management System that streamlines grassroots disaster reporting and automates National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC) SitRep consolidation for Local Government Units (LGUs) within a 5-month development lifecycle.

### Specific Objectives
To achieve the general objective, the research project is broken down into the following highly specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) objectives:

1. **User Management & Security:** 
   To develop a secure User Management Module with strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) that restricts confidential demographic and casualty data exclusively to authorized personnel (Purok Leaders, Barangay Officials, and City DRRMOs), achieving zero unauthorized access breaches and 100% RBAC compliance during pre-deployment security testing.

2. **Resilient Disaster Reporting Module:** 
   To develop an offline-first Disaster Reporting Module equipped with an automated SMS-fallback mechanism, enabling Purok Leaders to transmit geo-tagged hazard and damage data to the central server with a 100% transmission success rate even during severe telecom internet disruptions.

3. **Disaster Report Verification Module:** 
   To develop a Disaster Report Verification Module equipped with automated alert escalation, allowing City DRRMO personnel to instantly validate and triage incoming grassroots reports, effectively reducing the standard data consolidation latency from 6–12 hours to less than 15 minutes.

4. **Automated SitRep Generation Module:** 
   To develop a SitRep Generation Module that mathematically aggregates verified barangay demographic and infrastructure damage data and automatically populates the exact, official NDRRMC Situation Report template, ensuring 100% structural compliance with national DILG Operation LISTO reporting mandates.

5. **SitRep Catalog & Geo-Tagged Dashboard:** 
   To develop a SitRep Catalog and Dashboard Module that chronologically archives past reports and provides an interactive GIS heatmap visualization of disaster incidents, targeting a minimum System Usability Scale (SUS) score of 80/100 from LGU end-users during User Acceptance Testing (UAT).

6. **ISO/IEC 25010 System Evaluation:** 
   To quantitatively assess the overall software product quality of the developed system based on the ISO/IEC 25010 standards—specifically measuring Functional Suitability, Performance Efficiency, Usability, Reliability, and Security—aiming for an overall weighted mean acceptability rating of at least 4.0 ("Very Satisfactory") from a panel of IT experts and DRRM practitioners prior to final deployment.
